Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rotahaven (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the “App”). We are committed to respecting your privacy and complying with applicable US privacy laws, including but not limited to CCPA and CalOPPA.

We use the collected data to operate, maintain, and enhance Rotahaven. Specifically, to:

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in limited circumstances:

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Privacy Rights (US)

Depending on your state of residence (e.g., California, Virginia, Colorado), you may have the right to:

  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Delete your personal data (subject to legal exceptions).
  • Opt-out of the “sale” or sharing of personal information (we do not sell data).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the email below. We will respond within 45 days as required by law.

Rotahaven is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy within the app or via email. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use of Rotahaven after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
